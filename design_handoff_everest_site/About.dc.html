( Our journey )/ 01

25 years, one climb.

Founded in 2000, Everest Entertainment began with a vision to celebrate, preserve, and take Marathi stories to audiences everywhere. What started as a film distribution business has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading media and entertainment companies, spanning film production, distribution, television, music, digital content, and content aggregation.

Over the past 25 years, Everest has built one of India's largest repositories of Marathi entertainment, with a library of over 260 films, 50 iconic dramas (Nataks), and 800+ songs. Along the way, we have produced and partnered on award-winning films, collaborated with the industry's finest talent, and become the largest aggregator of Marathi content across television, digital, and OTT platforms.

As audiences and technology continue to evolve, so do we. Today, Everest's stories reach millions across theatres, television, YouTube, streaming platforms, and digital channels. The next chapter of our journey is JALLOSH — our consumer-facing brand that will bring together the Marathi entertainment ecosystem across FAST Channels, Amazon Prime Video Channels, OTT, and beyond, making the very best of Marathi entertainment accessible to audiences around the world.

For us, this isn't just the business of entertainment, it's a lifelong commitment to preserving, celebrating, and sharing the stories, music, and the Joy of Maharashtra with the world!
( Founder's note )/ 02
Fig. 01Founder

Sanjay Chhabria.

Cinema has been a part of my life for as long as I can remember. Growing up in a family deeply rooted in the film business, I had the privilege of witnessing not just the magic on screen, but the passion, perseverance, and belief it takes to bring every story to life. Those early experiences shaped my love for cinema and, eventually, inspired the journey that became Everest Entertainment.

When I founded Everest, my vision was simple, to build a home for Marathi storytelling. Over the years, that journey has evolved from film distribution into one of Maharashtra's leading media and entertainment companies, spanning film production, television, music, digital platforms, and one of India's largest libraries of Marathi content. Through every chapter, one belief has remained constant: great stories deserve to be discovered, preserved, and shared across generations.

The way people experience entertainment will continue to evolve from theatres to television, from streaming to connected devices, but the power of a meaningful story never changes. Our responsibility is to ensure these stories continue to find new audiences, wherever they are and however they choose to watch.

As we begin the next chapter with JALLOSH, our ambition is to create the Marathi entertainment ecosystem of the future, bringing together timeless classics, original films and series, theatre, music, and emerging formats to delight audiences around the world.

For me, Everest has never been just about building a media company. It has always been about creating a lasting home for Marathi stories and sharing the Joy of Marathi entertainment with audiences across generations and across the world.
Sanjay Chhabria
Founder, Everest Entertainment
