Over 260 films. One collection.

For over 25 years, Everest Entertainment has been at the forefront of Marathi cinema, building one of India's largest and most celebrated film libraries with over 260 titles.

Our collection spans timeless classics, National Award-winning films, blockbuster entertainers, historical epics, family favourites, and contemporary cinema, reflecting the rich legacy and evolving landscape of Marathi storytelling.

Our catalogue features some of the most iconic films in Marathi cinema, including Mee Shivajiraje Bhosale Boltoy, Tukaram, Pawankhind, Chandramukhi, Subhedar, Maharashtra Shaheer, the Mumbai Pune Mumbai franchise, the Boyz franchise (Boyz 2, Boyz 3, Boyz 4), Gulkand, Moraya, and Naach Ga Ghuma, among many others. It also proudly includes the complete film collection of the legendary DADA KONDKE, preserving the legacy of one of Marathi cinema's most iconic entertainers and ensuring that his timeless classics continue to delight audiences for generations to come.

Whether you're revisiting a beloved classic or discovering Marathi cinema for the very first time, every film in the Everest collection reflects our enduring commitment to preserving, celebrating, and sharing the very best of Marathi cinema with audiences across the world. Together, these films represent a living legacy — honouring the rich heritage of Marathi cinema while continuing to inspire and entertain new generations of audiences.

Iconic Nataks

50 iconic Nataks.

Marathi theatre has long been the foundation of Maharashtra's rich storytelling tradition, and Everest Entertainment is proud to preserve and showcase some of its most celebrated stage productions. Our growing collection of over 50 iconic Nataks brings together timeless classics, unforgettable performances, and enduring stories that continue to captivate audiences across generations.

From Vijay Tendulkar's legendary Sakharam Binder and Shantata! Court Chalu Aahe, to Prashant Damle's immensely popular Jadoo Teri Nazar & Char Divas Premache and Sharad Upadhye's acclaimed Rashichakra, our collection celebrates the finest voices, performers, and playwrights who have shaped Marathi theatre. Together, these productions preserve a remarkable theatrical legacy while introducing the magic of Marathi Nataks to audiences around the world.

Natak titles and artwork are being added to the site — get in touch for the full stage catalogue.
