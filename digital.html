( Every story, every screen )

From the theatre to the palm of your hand.

The internet has changed the way we watch movies. A whole generation discovers our films online first. Everest was among the first independent studios with a content output deal at Amazon — and we've kept building from there.

The slate streams across Amazon Prime Video, Netflix, JioCinema and Hungama, while our own YouTube home — Everest Marathi — has crossed 2 million subscribers, making it one of the leading Marathi channels for music, clips and short-form content. Films play on smart TVs, laptops, tablets and mobiles, with subtitles for global audiences.
