( The home of Marathi entertainment )/ 01

Same summit, new trail.

From timeless classics and blockbuster films to iconic Nataks, music, original series, devotional content, and exclusive programming, JALLOSH is designed to be the home of Marathi entertainment for audiences around the world.

Built on Everest's rich legacy and one of India's largest collections of Marathi content, JALLOSH seamlessly blends the stories audiences have cherished for generations with the convenience of modern entertainment. Whether you're revisiting a beloved classic, enjoying the latest blockbuster, or discovering something new, every experience is thoughtfully curated to celebrate the joy of being Marathi.
