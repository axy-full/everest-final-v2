Over 260 film titles, licensed and distributed across satellite, cable and broadcast partners — a deep, rights-cleared catalogue ready for any window. Decades of Marathi & Hindi cinema, organised, rights-cleared and ready to license — from marquee hits to evergreen library gems.

( Partners — taking Marathi entertainment everywhere )

For over 25 years, Everest Entertainment has been a trusted content partner to India's leading broadcasters, OTT platforms, and music streaming services, taking the very best of Marathi entertainment to audiences across India and around the world.

Our long-standing partnerships reflect the strength of our catalogue, the quality of our content, and our commitment to delivering exceptional entertainment across every platform. As the media landscape continues to evolve, Everest remains at the forefront of content distribution — working closely with leading broadcast, streaming, and music platforms while expanding our consumer footprint through JALLOSH, our upcoming Marathi entertainment ecosystem spanning FAST Channels, Amazon Prime Video Channels, Smart TVs, and OTT.
